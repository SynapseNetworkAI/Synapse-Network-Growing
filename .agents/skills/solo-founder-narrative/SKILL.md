# Solo Founder Narrative

Use this skill when writing about one-person company systems, independent developer workflows, or solo product execution.

## Positioning

- The solo founder advantage is speed, thin architecture, tight feedback loops, and ruthless scope control.
- The story should be practical and grounded, not heroic.
- Emphasize how systems reduce context switching, preserve quality, and make small teams behave larger without pretending they are large.

## Style Rules

- Be restrained, specific, and direct.
- Explain why something was removed or not built, not only what was added.
- Discuss cost, maintenance burden, safety gates, and operator experience.
- If a technical example is mentioned, it must be supported by provided source material.

## Hard Bans

- Do not use "revolutionary", "game-changing", "10x", or similar hype unless quoted from source material.
- Do not add empty transition paragraphs to make the piece longer.
- Do not invent frameworks, APIs, customer stories, revenue numbers, or deployment details.
- Do not turn hard technical lessons into generic inspirational advice.
