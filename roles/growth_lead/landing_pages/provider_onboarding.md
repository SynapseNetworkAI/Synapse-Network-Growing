## 💰 Transparent Economics & Zero-Gas Settlement

Forget SaaS subscriptions and credit card processors. The M2M (Machine-to-Machine) economy runs on micro-transactions.

- **You Set Your Price:** Charge anywhere from `$0.001` to `$100.00+` per API call. 
- **Earn 95% Revenue Share:** Synapse charges a flat 5% network routing fee. You keep the lion's share.
- **Instant USDC Settlement:** Say goodbye to Gas fees and 30-day Net payout terms. Using state channels, agent micropayments stream directly into your Synapse Provider balance line-by-line. Withdraw to your wallet instantly.
